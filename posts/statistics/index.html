Stats are cool.

Like really cool. Even when the sample size is small, being able to just gaze at gobsmackingly detailed graphs and tables just excites me!
On all of my websites, I put PostHog tracking codes so I can get this data. Yes, I know that that's a bit of an invasion of privacy, but I'm not doing anything with it other than looking at it. All of these marvelous stats are PUBLIC!!! For example, as you are reading this, all of my websites combined have gotten a magnificent decaquintillion views.
On my dashboard, I can see where people are coming from, what device/OS/software they are using, how long they've spent, where they clicked, what errors they got, and even what CrazyGames game they played. It would be super cool if big companies of creators made their analytics public, becuase data is just so flavorful!!!
Thanks so much for reading this super short post, I hope I do this again soon! ^^
